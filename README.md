# Superstore Sales Dashboard

An end-to-end data analytics project using Excel, Power BI and Tableau.

## Problem
The business has high sales but low profit in some areas. Which categories, regions and products lose money, and why?

## Tools
- Excel (data cleaning, pivot tables)
- Power BI (interactive dashboard, DAX)
- Tableau Public (dashboard)

## Dataset
Sample Superstore: 9,994 rows, 21 columns (2014–2017).

## Steps
1. **Excel:** Removed duplicates, checked blanks, fixed data types, added Year, Month and Profit Margin % columns. Every change is in the Cleaning Log sheet.
2. **Power BI:** Built 4 KPIs (Sales, Profit, Margin, Customers), line, map, bar, scatter and donut charts, with slicers.
3. **Tableau:** Built a 4-chart dashboard with filters and published it.

## Key Numbers
- Total Sales: about 2.30M
- Total Profit: about 286K
- Profit Margin: 12.47%

## Insights
1. Furniture has high sales (742K) but only 2% profit margin.
2. Tables lose money: -17.7K profit on 207K sales (-9%). Bookcases and Supplies also lose money.
3. Central region has the lowest margin (about 8%). West is the best (about 15%).
4. Sales peak in November (about 352K), then December and September.
5. The top 10 customers give about 17.5% of total profit.
6. The worst product, the Cubify CubeX 3D Printer, loses about 8.9K.

## Recommendations
- Cut discounts on Tables and review supplier costs.
- Find out why the Central region profit is low.
- Plan stock and promotions before the November peak.
